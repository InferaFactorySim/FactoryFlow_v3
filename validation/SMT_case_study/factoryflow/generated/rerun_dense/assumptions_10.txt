1.  The `screen_printer`'s special behavior of a 28-unit cleaning cycle after every second board is not a standard machine parameter and is assumed to be implemented using a custom processing delay function that tracks the part count.
2.  The service intervals for all `ServicedMachine` nodes (`screen_printer`, `pick_and_place_1`, `pick_and_place_2`) are assumed to be based on the number of parts processed since the last service.
3.  The descriptive power states are assumed to map to the simulation framework's internal states. This includes: `processing` or `printing` to `PROCESSING_STATE`, `idle` to `STARVED_STATE`, `waiting_to_output` to `BLOCKED_STATE`, and `waiting_for_refill` or `waiting_for_reel_replacement` to `WAITING_FOR_OPERATOR_STATE`.
4.  For the `reflow_oven`, the `warm-up` power rating is applied during its initial `node_setup_time`. The `temperature maintained` power rating is applied during all subsequent active states, as no power-down condition is specified.
5.  The `work_capacity` for `line_loader`, `screen_printer`, `pick_and_place_1`, `pick_and_place_2`, and `buffering_module` is assumed to be 1, as this parameter was not specified for these nodes.
6.  The total transit time for a part on a conveyor belt is assumed to be the `delay` per slot multiplied by the belt's `capacity` (3 time units total).
