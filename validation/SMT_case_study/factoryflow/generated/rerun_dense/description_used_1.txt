TOP (SystemModel) is an automated SMT-PCB assembly line: a single linear flow of 5 processing machines from a batch-fed source to a sink, with one shared human operator. TOP holds 1 BatchSource source_1 (batch_size=16, batches_ahead=1, inter_arrival_time=0, blocking=True; 16 boards are created at one instant sharing one creation timestamp and released one at a time, with one further stack prepared while the current one is fed), 1 Machine line_loader (processing_delay=2), 1 ServicedMachine screen_printer (processing_delay=18 per board plus a 28 cleaning cycle after every 2nd board; services=[solder_refill interval 100, adhesive_refill interval 100]), 1 ServicedMachine pick_and_place_1 (processing_delay=85, services=[reel_replacement interval 50]), 1 ServicedMachine pick_and_place_2 (processing_delay=50, services=[reel_replacement interval 50]), 1 Machine buffering_module (processing_delay=0, pass-through in this configuration), 1 Machine reflow_oven (work_capacity=10, processing_delay=50, node_setup_time=3600), 1 Sink sink_1, and 1 HumanOperator operator_1 (capacity=1; tasks solder_refill@screen_printer=60, adhesive_refill@screen_printer=60, reel_replacement@pick_and_place_1=60, reel_replacement@pick_and_place_2=60; a machine whose service falls due stalls until the operator is free and the task completes, requests queueing FIFO). 5 Buffers (capacity=1 each): b0 source_1->line_loader, b1 line_loader->screen_printer, b2 pick_and_place_1->pick_and_place_2, b3 pick_and_place_2->buffering_module, b4 reflow_oven->sink_1. 2 ConveyorBelts (capacity=3 slots, delay=1 per slot, non-accumulating so the whole belt stalls if the last slot is not collected): belt_sp_pp1 screen_printer->pick_and_place_1, belt_bm_rfo buffering_module->reflow_oven. Total: 1 source + 5 machines + 1 pass-through + 1 sink = 8 flow nodes plus 1 operator; 5 buffers + 2 belts = 7 edges. pick_and_place_1 at 85 is the bottleneck; the oven holds 10 boards at once for 50 each and must complete its 3600 warm-up before the first board. Power ratings in watts, by component state: screen_printer idle=100, waiting_for_refill=100, printing=500, cleaning=1000, waiting_to_output=100; pick_and_place_1 and pick_and_place_2 idle=100, waiting_for_reel_replacement=100, processing=500, waiting_to_output=100; buffering_module=250 in every state; reflow_oven off=320, warm-up=33000, temperature maintained=25800 whether occupied or not. line_loader, both conveyor belts and the transfer positions draw no power and are excluded from the energy total, which is therefore taken over the screen printer, both pick and place machines, the buffering module and the reflow oven.
